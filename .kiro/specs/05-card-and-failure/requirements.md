# Saved card and honest failure

BORRADOR-PARA-KIRO / DRAFT-FOR-KIRO — 2026-11-03. Author: Codex Central. Not generated or approved by Kiro; not implementation. Derived from DOSSIER-2026-11-02 §4. Jonás decides open product choices.

## Requirements

### CARD-1
WHEN a user saves a card, THE SYSTEM SHALL retain source identity, retrieval date, document version/hash, literal references and classification version.

### CARD-2
WHEN reading or classification fails, THE SYSTEM SHALL report the failure and SHALL NOT show a successful analysis.

### CARD-3
THE SYSTEM SHALL support the public, no-account reading journey without mandatory server processing.

### CARD-4
WHEN saved terms change, THE SYSTEM SHALL distinguish an observed change from an unchecked version; it SHALL NOT claim background monitoring that does not run.
