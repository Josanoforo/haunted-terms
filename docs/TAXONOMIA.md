## Classification contract proposed for Kiro

Return the exact quote, document identifier and hash, start/end offsets with encoding convention, surrounding exception span, category candidate, cited source and uncertainty reason. A matching phrase is not proof of abusiveness. Preserve local-law savings, notice/refund conditions, whether the user or provider caused the harm, and country/service scope. No keyword may create a final legal finding on its own.

When applicability, statutory deadline, clause boundaries, exceptions or source integrity are unresolved, use LC00 and show exactly: “no puedo decirte si esto es abusivo”, together with the literal. Never substitute a dramatic severity for uncertainty. Severity scale and category-to-severity rules remain a proposal for Jonás/Kiro review; none are approved here. Candidate critical status is for study preparation, not the severity label.

No automatic contract enforcement advice, no hidden legal verdict. Renewal and data-use categories can be proposed later with their own sources; they are not invented as article 90 fractions.
